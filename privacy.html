Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Horizon Advance LLC ("Horizon Advance," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or contact us about business funding, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us: your name, email address, phone number, business name, industry, time in business, revenue, funding needs, and anything else you include in your messages.
  • Documents you provide: bank statements, tax returns, financial statements, identification, and other materials requested to evaluate funding options.
  • Information from third parties: business and personal credit reports, public records, and identity verification results, obtained with your authorization where required.
  • Usage information: browser and device type, pages visited, and referring sites, collected through cookies and similar technologies.

The Find Your Fit tool on our website runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are not sent to us unless you choose to email them.

How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiries and evaluate which funding programs fit your business
  • To prepare and present funding options and offers
  • To verify identity and prevent fraud
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations
  • To improve our website and services

How we share information

We share information with lenders, funding partners, and SBA-approved lenders as needed to evaluate and provide financing; with service providers who help us operate our business; with credit bureaus and verification services; and with authorities when required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

Calls and text messages

With your consent, we may call or text you about your funding options. You can opt out of texts at any time by replying STOP. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Cookies

We use cookies to operate the site, understand how it is used, and measure advertising. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Security and retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information, and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To make a request, contact us using the details below.
