SBA loan programs

SBA loans, without the maze.

SBA loans take longer, but the terms are hard to beat. We'll tell you up front whether you're a strong candidate, and which program fits.

Most popular

SBA 7(a)

The SBA's flagship program and the most flexible. It can be used for nearly any business purpose.

  • Working capital, equipment, and inventory
  • Buying a business or a partner's share
  • Refinancing existing business debt
  • Terms up to 10 years, or 25 for real estate
Real estate & equipment

SBA 504

Long-term, fixed-rate financing for major fixed assets that help your business grow.

  • Buying or building commercial property
  • Heavy machinery and long-life equipment
  • Down payments often around 10%
  • Terms of 10, 20, or 25 years
Faster decisions

SBA Express

A streamlined version of 7(a) for smaller amounts, with a quicker turnaround on the lender's decision.

  • Loans up to $500,000
  • Can be structured as a term loan or revolving line
  • Less paperwork than a standard 7(a)
  • Good for working capital and smaller projects

SBA loans are made by SBA-approved lenders and partially guaranteed by the U.S. Small Business Administration. Approval is up to the lender and the SBA. Horizon Advance is not affiliated with or endorsed by the SBA.

Frequently asked questions

Which program is right for my business?

A quick rule of thumb: if you need money this week, look at a merchant cash advance. If you have a specific project with a clear payoff, consider a term loan. For ongoing cash-flow gaps, a line of credit works best. For big, long-term moves where you can wait, an SBA loan. Try the Find Your Fit tool, or just email us and we'll tell you.

Can I qualify for more than one program?

Often, yes. Many businesses qualify for two or three options at once. We'll lay them out side by side so you can compare the real cost and payments before you choose.

What documents will I need?

For a merchant cash advance or line of credit, usually three to six months of business bank statements and a photo ID. Term loans and SBA loans typically also need business tax returns and financial statements. SBA loans may ask for a debt schedule and a short business plan.

How fast can I get funded?

A merchant cash advance can fund in as little as 24 to 48 hours. Term loans usually take a few business days, and lines of credit are often approved in one to three days. SBA loans are the slowest at roughly 30 to 90 days, but they offer the longest terms and some of the lowest rates.

Is a merchant cash advance a loan?

No. A merchant cash advance is a purchase of a portion of your future receivables, priced with a factor rate instead of an interest rate. You'll see the total amount to be repaid before you sign.

Is Horizon Advance part of the SBA?

No. SBA loans are made by SBA-approved lenders and partially guaranteed by the U.S. Small Business Administration. Horizon Advance is not affiliated with or endorsed by the SBA. We help you understand the programs and pursue the right one.

Does it cost anything to talk to you?

No. Reaching out and reviewing your options is free, and there's no obligation to accept any offer.
